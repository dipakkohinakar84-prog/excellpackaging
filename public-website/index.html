About Us

Excell Packaging is built around practical industrial packaging needs.

Since 2011, the company has worked with industrial customers who need packaging that can support movement, protection, storage and dispatch. The focus is straightforward: understand the product, choose the right packaging format and supply consistently.

From wooden pallets and crates to corrugated boxes, PE film and aluminium foil packaging, Excell Packaging supports requirements across manufacturing, engineering, warehousing, logistics and export packaging.

  • Industrial-grade packaging range
  • Custom sizing and practical material selection
  • Manufacturing support across Maharashtra and Gujarat
